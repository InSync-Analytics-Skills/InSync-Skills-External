# KPI spines by sub-industry

The scorecard's "Top Operating KPIs" band and the evolution table's band B come from the company's own disclosed drivers.
Discovery order: (1) the Earnings Preview Portal's Summary Financials rows for the name, if one exists; (2) `search_lineitem filter:"top_kpi"`;
(3) filter-removed `search_lineitem` for the sector list below; (4) the release's operating tables (parse and add to `facts` if InSync has no series).
Pick 5-8 KPIs. Every KPI row needs: five quarters of actuals (nine if it gets a Y/Y sub-row), consensus where any exists (flag thin), prior and new guide where guided.

Rules that apply everywhere
- Choose the row by what the company does, not by `insync_subindustry`: the label can be wrong or too broad. State the spine row used in the delivery note.
- Lead the KPI band with the metric management guides and the Street models most (deliveries and orders for a builder; cRPO for SaaS; comps for retail).
- Rates (margins, ratios) are `kind:"rate"` → surprises and Y/Y in bps. Levels are `kind:"level"` → percent.
- A KPI without any consensus stays in the table with a dash in the consensus column; the comment says "no consensus series".
- A KPI the company stopped reporting is dropped, with a sentence in Source.
- Never lead a table with absolute revenue, EBITDA or EPS as if they were operating drivers; they sit in the financial band.
